Farsi-Joon — Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Farsi-Joon and tap the Settings gear icon in the top-right corner.
  2. Scroll to Account & Data Safety and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm deletion. This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account, Cloud Firestore profile, lesson progress, daily chat quota, chat reports, and local progress/recordings.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, email siavashstara@gmail.com from your Google account email with the subject "Farsi-Joon Account Deletion Request". All associated Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
