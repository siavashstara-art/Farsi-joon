Privacy Policy — Farsi Joon

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Farsi Joon ("the App") is a Persian language learning application designed for learners aged 13 and older.

1. Data Stored Locally Only

  • Offline Lessons, Vocabulary & Leitner Progress: Saved locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore.
  • Speaking Practice Microphone Recordings: When you grant the optional RECORD_AUDIO permission in Speak Practice, your voice is recorded temporarily in your app cache (.m4a) exclusively for on-device playback. Recordings are never uploaded to any server.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Services

  • Authentication: Optional Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication & Credential Manager) is required only to unlock cloud progress backup and the AI Persian Chat Tutor.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores your Firebase UID, display name, lesson completion stars/scores, daily chat message count (max 10/day), and user-submitted AI response reports.
  • AI Persian Chat Tutor (Firebase AI Logic / Gemini): Prompts you explicitly send are transmitted securely over HTTPS with Firebase App Check attestation to generate Persian conversation replies.

3. Data Sharing & Advertising

Farsi Joon does not sell personal data, does not share personal data with third-party advertisers, and contains no ad SDKs.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account and all associated cloud and local records anytime inside the app via Settings → Delete Account, or follow instructions on our Account Deletion page.
